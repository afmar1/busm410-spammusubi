# Sales Project Notes

## Project overview
This project focuses on analyzing liquor sales data from Iowa using Google Cloud and BigQuery. The goal is to explore sales trends, identify patterns, and produce insights that can support business-oriented reporting and analysis.

## Team
- Team name: SpamMusubi
- Members: Algie and Sia

## Dataset
- Source: Iowa Liquor Sales
- BigQuery dataset: `bigquery-public-data.iowa_liquor_sales`
- Project ID: `salesproject-511800`

## Objectives
- Understand the structure and fields available in the Iowa liquor sales dataset.
- Explore key sales metrics such as volume, revenue, category, and geographic trends.
- Identify patterns by product type, date, and location.
- Prepare analyses and summaries that can be used for reporting or presentation.

## Notes
- Data will be queried and processed in BigQuery.
- The project should maintain a clear record of assumptions, queries, and findings.
- Results should be organized so they can be shared effectively with the team and stakeholders.
